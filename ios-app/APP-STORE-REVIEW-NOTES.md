## 3. Age rating answers

Answer No to every question about violence, sex, drugs, gambling, horror and
contests. Answer Yes only to:

- Unrestricted Web Access: No (the app only opens ontimetaxi.biz)
- Does your app include user generated content? Yes - riders and drivers can
  send each other messages. Say the app has a reporting tool and the owner
  can suspend accounts, because both are true.

Expected result: 4+.

## 4. App Privacy answers

Say Yes, the app collects data, then tick:

- Contact Info: name, email address, phone number - used for App
  Functionality. Linked to the user. Not used for tracking.
- Location: precise location - used for App Functionality. Linked to the
  user. Not used for tracking.
- User Content: photos, audio, messages - used for App Functionality.
  Linked to the user. Not used for tracking.
- Identifiers: user ID - used for App Functionality. Linked to the user.
- Financial Info: payment information is handled entirely by Stripe and is
  never stored by On Time Taxi. Declare "Purchase History" only, for App
  Functionality.

Answer No to "Do you or your third-party partners use data for tracking?".

Account deletion: Yes. The in-app link is
https://ontimetaxi.biz/delete-account
